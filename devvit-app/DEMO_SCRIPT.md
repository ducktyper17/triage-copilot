Voiceover word-for-word. Frame-by-frame video plan.
Total: 60 seconds. Stay under 1:00 or judges stop watching.

## 0:00 – 0:08 — The pain (8s)

**On screen:**
A Reddit mod queue with 50+ items. Mod clicks one item, then opens a new tab to check the user's profile, then another tab for the mod log, then a third tab for the sub's rules. Tabs piling up. Cursor frantic.

**Voiceover:**
> "Reddit moderators spend over twenty hours a week clicking through items like this. To decide on a single report, they open five different tabs."

## 0:08 – 0:18 — The hidden pain (10s)

**On screen:**
Split-screen: Mod Alice opens the SAME item. Mod Bob, in a different window, opens the same item. Neither knows the other is there. Both type a removal reason. Both hit Remove. The post gets removed twice. Wasted work.

**Voiceover:**
> "And seventy-four percent of mods say they constantly bump into each other doing the same work — because no Reddit tool tells them another mod is already on it. Until now."

## 0:18 – 0:35 — The Claim moment (the hero shot) (17s)

**On screen:**
Same item, now with Triage installed. Mod Bob (left window) clicks **Triage with Copilot**. The modal opens with a green banner at the top:

> ✅ YOU CLAIMED THIS ITEM
> Reserved for you for the next 300s.

Quick cut: Mod Alice (right window), seconds later, clicks **Triage with Copilot** on the same item. Her modal opens with a red banner:

> 🚫 CLAIMED BY ANOTHER MOD
> u/Bob claimed this item 12s ago.
> Pick a different queue item — or check Override below to proceed anyway.

Alice's cursor closes the modal — she moves to the next item in her queue.

**Voiceover:**
> "Triage adds claim-and-release to Reddit moderation. The first mod to open an item claims it for five minutes. The second mod is told to move on. Redundant work, eliminated."

**On screen:**
Mod ticks three checkboxes — Remove + Ban 7 days + Reply with reason. Clicks **Apply**. In a single motion: the post disappears, the user gets banned, a distinguished mod reply posts on the comment thread, the mod log records all three actions.

**Voiceover:**
> "Tick the boxes you want. Hit Apply. Triage runs the whole action chain — remove, ban, reply, lock — in one submit. What used to take four pages takes one."

## Recording notes

- Use macOS **QuickTime → New Screen Recording** (or `Cmd+Shift+5`)
- Record at 1920x1080 if your screen supports it, otherwise native resolution
- Two browser windows visible simultaneously for the split-screen at 0:08
- Use simple cursor (no Highlighter / annotations) — keep it Reddit-realistic
- Voiceover: record separately if your environment is noisy, then dub in iMovie
- Export as MP4 (H.264). Final file < 50 MB so it uploads to YouTube quickly
- Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** — paste the link into the Devpost submission
